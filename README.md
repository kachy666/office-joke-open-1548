# Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232` — one
single-line joke about office life, at most 200 characters, judged head to
head by an AI judge. $2 to the single funniest joke.

## The joke

> Our daily standup has lasted 47 minutes, and not one of us is standing.

71 characters, one line. The twist lands in the last clause: a "standup"
meeting where nobody is standing.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints the joke and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`
when set). The entry is validated first: 1–200 characters, a single line,
no leading or trailing whitespace.
